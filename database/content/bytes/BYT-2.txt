The word "recovery" promises a return to how things were. But what if the "normal" you're trying to get back to after a major challenge no longer exists?

**Recovery is a Rebirth, Not a Rewind.**
True healing isn’t about rewinding the clock to before an illness or hardship. That version of you is in the past. Every significant challenge, physical or emotional, leaves its mark—a memory in your body and a shift in your mind. The real recovery isn't just from the disease, but from the mental "dis-ease" that comes with it: the fear, the anxiety, the uncertainty. This struggle holds a hidden gift. By consciously working through it, you don't just return to who you were; you get the chance to be reborn into someone stronger, more aware, and more resilient.

**Your Practice:**
Take 60 seconds to reframe a past challenge. Think of a difficult time you went through. Instead of dwelling on what you lost, ask yourself: "What new strength did I uncover because of this?" See the event not as a wound, but as a springboard.

**A Final Thought:**
Healing isn’t about erasing the past. It’s about using its lessons to build a more conscious you.
