It is easy to look at the world and see a planet fractured by an us against them mentality. But what if that deep sense of division isn't just a symptom of our problems, but the actual root cause of them all?
 
 The core insight here is that the biggest global dysfunction from poverty and conflict to political hostility all stem from one foundational belief: the **Myth of Separation**. We operate as if we are completely separate individuals, nations, and groups. This illusion makes it possible to see others' problems as "not our own," allowing us to tolerate widespread suffering and injustice. Think of it like a body where the hand believes it's separate from the heart; eventually, the entire system fails because it has forgotten it's part of a single, interconnected whole.
 
 The solution, then, is a conscious shift to a mindset of **Oneness**. This doesn't mean erasing our unique identities. It means choosing to see ourselves as individual expressions of one single essence one human family. When you truly believe that the well-being of others is tied to your own, your motivations and actions naturally change. You can't ignore a starving child or a polluted river, because you recognize it's happening to a part of *you*. This single decision is the switch that can move us from a culture of competition to one of cooperation.
 
 Here is a simple practice for today. Take 60 seconds to reflect on this question: Do you see yourself primarily as a separate individual navigating the world alone, or as an essential part of a larger, interconnected whole? Don't judge your answer. Just notice how each perspective feels.
 
 Changing the world begins not with a grand external movement, but with the quiet, internal decision of who we choose to be, together.
