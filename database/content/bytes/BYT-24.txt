That moment a child's eyes light up with pure curiosity is one of parenting's greatest rewards. But what if our main job isn't to fill their heads with facts, but to nurture their ability to listen to their own heart?

### Core Insight: Guide Them to Their Inner Compass

Parenting is less about a checklist of duties and more about fostering a child’s inner guidance system. You can do this by turning everyday life into a discovery zone that feeds their natural sense of wonder—whether it's looking at stars, planting seeds, or learning about different cultures. This approach also means not shielding them from life's complexities. By gently introducing concepts like resilience, loss, and even global problems when they're ready, you equip them with empathy and strength. The ultimate goal isn't to raise a child who just chases external success, but one who learns to look within, trust their own feelings, and navigate the world with a confident and courageous heart.

### Your Practice This Week

Find one "wonder moment" with a child in your life. It could be as simple as stopping to look at an interesting bug on the sidewalk, watching the clouds, or asking them what they're curious about right now. Instead of giving a quick answer, share in their discovery. Ask questions back: "What do you think it's doing?" or "Why do you think the sky is that color?" Spend just a few minutes exploring the question together, without any goal other than to share in the curiosity.

### The Takeaway

The greatest gift you can give a child isn't your wisdom, but the confidence to trust their own.
