The moment a child is born, a parent's dream for them begins to take shape. But what happens when our vision for their life clashes with the path they’re meant to walk?

### You're a Custodian, Not an Owner

Think of yourself less as a manager of your child's life and more as a trusted custodian of their unique journey. Your primary role isn't to build their future for them, but to provide a solid foundation of values—like love, trust, truthfulness, and self-respect. Just like a gardener can't force a seed to become a specific flower but can provide the perfect soil and sun for it to flourish, your job is to create a nurturing environment. When you provide this strong grounding, you empower your children to grow into the person they are meant to be, not the person you expect them to be.

### Let Go of Expectations to Build Connection

One of the hardest lessons in parenting is learning to release your own dreams for your children. When we impose our expectations—about their career, their choices, their personality—we unintentionally create a recipe for conflict and disappointment. The real strength lies in letting go of control. Instead of trying to steer their ship, become their lighthouse. Be the steady, guiding light they can always look to, knowing you’re there to support them no matter which way the seas turn. This means truly communicating: listening to understand them, not just hearing their words.

### Your Practice

Take a moment today and identify one specific expectation you hold for your child. It could be big or small. Now, try to reframe it. Instead of focusing on a specific outcome (like "I want them to be an A-student"), shift to a foundational value ("I want them to love learning and feel confident in their efforts"). This small shift moves you from a position of control to one of support.

### A Final Thought

Parenting isn't about carving a path *for* your children. It’s about handing them a compass of love and faith so they can navigate their own incredible voyage.
