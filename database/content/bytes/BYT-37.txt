Why can a person at the gym build more muscle in an hour than a farm worker who toils all day? It's not just about the work, but the attitude. The same principle applies to your own growth: the method you use is only 5% of the equation. The other 95% is the energy and intention you bring to it.

***

### **Attitude Is Your Engine**

Simply going through the motions of a practice like meditation can be counterproductive. If you sit down feeling negative or that it's a chore, you're just meditating on negativity, which only makes it stronger. The key is to approach your practice with the same excitement you'd feel about meeting someone you love. This enthusiasm—this genuine interest in connecting with your inner self—is what turns a mechanical routine into a life-changing experience.

### **Let Go of Expectations**

Many of us meditate hoping for a specific outcome, like a feeling of peace or a repeat of a past profound experience. But this creates a condition that can block something even better from happening. Your practice works best when you demand nothing and simply wait with openness. Just as you can't force a butterfly out of its cocoon without harming it, you can't rush your own growth. Trust the process and accept whatever comes, even if it feels mundane. Every experience has a purpose.

### **Embrace Joyful Acceptance**

In relationships and life, true acceptance isn't just gritting your teeth and tolerating things. It’s a cheerful, wholehearted embrace of reality. This means accepting your own faults, the quirks of others, and life’s unexpected challenges with a courageous heart. When you can joyfully accept what is, you stop fighting reality and allow beauty to emerge from even the most difficult situations.

***

### **Try This Today**

Take a moment to reflect on two questions. First: "How well do I accept my own faults?" Second: "How well do I accept the faults of others and the circumstances around me?" Don't judge your answers. Simply notice what comes up with full awareness.

***

Everyone thinks of changing the world, but the real work starts within. Your attitude is the first and most powerful tool you have to design your own destiny and, in turn, help shape a better world.
