There's a good reason you might feel sluggish or off-balance, even when you think you're eating well. The secret might not be in cutting things out, but in creating a better internal balance.
 
 Here's how to get started.
 
 **1. Aim for a 75/25 Balance.**
 Think of your body like a scale. Too many acidic foods like sugar, meat, coffee, and processed snacks can tip it out of balance, leading to issues with digestion, skin, and mood. The goal is not to eliminate these foods entirely, but to ensure that about 75% of what you eat is alkaline-forming. This means filling your plate with mostly plant-based foods like vegetables, pulses, and whole grains. This simple ratio helps your body find its natural equilibrium without feeling restrictive.
 
 **2. Your Thoughts Create Acidity, Too.**
 What you consume isn't limited to your plate. Stress, anxiety, and negative emotions can flood your system with even more acid than a cup of coffee. This means that managing your mental state is just as crucial for your physical well-being as the food you eat. Simple practices like meditation or deep breathing exercises can help regulate your nervous system, calming the internal acidic environment and improving everything from tension to sleep.
 
 ### Your Practice This Week
 
 Focus on adding just one of these four powerful alkaline boosters to your daily routine. You can start your day with a big glass of water, add a spoonful of flax or chia seeds (for omega oils) to your meal, mix a little liquid chlorophyll into your water, or swap your table salt for a natural sea salt. Pick one and see how you feel.
 
 **True wellness isn't about restriction; it's about creating harmony, both on your plate and in your mind.**
