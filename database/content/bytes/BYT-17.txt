There’s a powerful way to practice generosity that feels less like a duty and more like a fun, undercover game. It’s a simple challenge that shifts your focus from getting credit to just enjoying the act of giving.

### **Core Insight: Become a Secret Agent of Kindness**

The idea is to turn kindness into a secret mission. You anonymously choose one person in your life—a friend, family member, or colleague—to be your "target." For the next week, your mission is to secretly perform small, kind acts for them each day. The goal is to brighten their day without them ever knowing it was you. This gamified approach removes the pressure of being recognized and instead focuses you on the pure, intrinsic joy of making someone else feel good.

### **Your Practice: Accept the Mission**

Ready to try it? Here’s your assignment for the next three days.

1.  **Choose Your Target:** Silently pick one person. Don't tell anyone who it is.
2.  **Perform Secret Acts:** Each day, do one small, kind thing for them without being discovered. You could leave an anonymous note of appreciation on their desk, secretly do a small chore they normally handle, or buy them their favorite coffee and have it left for them.
3.  **Stay Undercover:** The whole point is the mystery. Observe from a distance and enjoy knowing you made a positive impact.

### **Final Thought**

True generosity isn't about the recognition you receive. It's about knowing you made someone's world a little brighter, even if they never know it was you.
