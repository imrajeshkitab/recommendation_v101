There are moments in life that feel like being lost in a dark wood, where the way forward is completely blotted out. In our modern, spinning world, it’s easy to feel overwhelmed and alone in that chaos. But what if you could find a point of stillness right in the center of it all?

### **Join a Network of Stillness**

The poet T.S. Eliot wrote about the “still point of the turning world”—a place of quiet amidst the noise. But today, you don’t have to find that stillness alone. Think of every person who takes a moment to pause and meditate as a single point of light. When you meditate, especially with a group, you’re not just finding your own calm; you’re plugging into a connected matrix of these still points all over the world. This creates a powerful sense of community and belonging, reminding you that you’re part of a vast network of people seeking the same peace.

### **Build Your Inner Refuge**

Meditation is like building a secret inner world that you can access anytime, especially when life gets stressful. It’s a place that strengthens and stabilizes you from the inside out. When you’re facing a difficult day at work or a personal challenge, you can close your eyes and visit this internal refuge. It’s a space that reminds you, "This too shall pass," and helps you realize you have the strength to endure. This isn’t about escaping reality; it’s about tapping into a source of eternal energy that makes your world feel calmer, less dramatic, and full of light.

### **Practice: The Five-Minute Pause**

You don’t need to be an expert to begin. For your practice, simply find five minutes today to sit still. Don’t worry about “emptying your mind” or doing it perfectly. Let your thoughts about your to-do list, your day, or your dinner plans come and go. The goal isn’t to stop thinking; it’s to stop being rushed and harried for a few conscious moments. Just rest.

### **Closing Thought**

Finding stillness isn’t about doing nothing. It’s about creating the space to connect with an inner strength that makes you wiser, lighter, and more resilient for everything else you do.
