Picture a forest. You probably see trees, animals, and dirt. But what if that’s just the surface? What if the real forest is an invisible field of energy you can step into, a place you can only experience by feeling it?

### The True State of Things

The world around you—the people, places, and objects—are more than just their physical forms. Think of them as expressions of a deeper, underlying energy. Just as a forest is more than a collection of trees, your reality is more than what your eyes can see. The true nature of things is a field of feeling, a silent harmony. To connect with it, you have to shift from just looking to truly sensing. This is how you move beyond the surface and experience the profound sense of connection that exists beneath it all.

### Enter Through the Heart

This deeper connection isn't something you can think your way into; it's a journey you take with your heart. It requires you to let go of analysis and allow yourself to be gently taken in by the feeling of a moment. When you do, you might find that you feel completely at home, even in unfamiliar places. This is the feeling of being held by the world’s energy—a sense of belonging that reminds you that you are a significant part of a vast, interconnected whole. It’s a quiet, loving embrace that’s always there, waiting for you to feel it.

### Your Practice

Sometime today, find a natural object—a houseplant, a tree outside your window, or even a stone. Instead of just looking at it, close your eyes for 60 seconds. Rest a hand on it or near it. Quiet your mind and try to sense its presence. Don't search for a specific feeling; just be open to whatever you notice. Is there stillness? A subtle vibration? A sense of calm? Simply feel, without judgment.

### The Takeaway

True connection isn't about what you see. It's about feeling the invisible embrace that holds everything, and everyone, together.
