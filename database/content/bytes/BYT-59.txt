Your conscious mind the part of you reading this right now is just a sliver of your total awareness, like a thin film between two vast oceans. Understanding what lies in those depths is the key to unlocking a more profound way of living.
 
 Here's the distilled wisdom on expanding your consciousness:
 
 **1. Your Mind Operates on Three Levels at Once.**
 Think of your awareness as having three layers that are always interacting. Below the surface is the **subconscious**, an ocean of past experiences, fears, and desires that constantly shapes your present feelings and reactions. The surface level is your **conscious** mind, the part that's alert and making decisions. And above it all is the **superconscious**, a source of unexpected insight, intuition, and inspiration. You're never operating on just one level; your gut feelings, sudden creative ideas, and old habits are all part of a dynamic interplay happening every single moment.
 
 **2. True Awareness is a 360-Degree View, Not a Narrow Focus.**
 The goal of evolving your consciousness isn't to zone out or achieve a stone-like trance. it is to develop what yogis call *sahaj sahaj samadhi a state of 360-degree awareness. This is where you can be engaged in your work while also being aware of your inner state, the environment around you, and a deeper connection to everything. it is about moving your center of operations from the individual-focused mind to the collective-focused heart, where a much wider, more integrated perception becomes possible.
 
 **A Simple Practice**
 
 The next time you face a small, everyday decision like what to eat for lunch or whether to make a phone call pause for just 30 seconds. Ask yourself: Where is this impulse coming from? Is it a familiar habit or craving from the past (subconscious)? Is it a purely logical, practical choice (conscious)? Or is there a quiet, intuitive nudge you can not quite explain (superconscious)? do not judge the answer; just notice.
 
 **A Final Thought**
 
 Expanding your awareness isn't about escaping the world. It is about learning to experience it more completely, with access to the wisdom of your past and the inspiration for your future, all at once.
