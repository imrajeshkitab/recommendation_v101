Imagine being so absorbed in a project that hours fly by, meals are forgotten, but instead of feeling drained, you feel energized and completely light. This isn't about working less; it's about changing the *way* you work.

The secret to this effortless state is letting go of the invisible baggage we usually carry. Think of your typical work stress—worrying if you're good enough, getting irritated by others, or taking things personally—as a heavy backpack. When you immerse yourself in a task with a clear sense of purpose, you unconsciously set that backpack down. The work itself might still be intense, but without the weight of ego, justification, or guilt, your energy can flow freely. This is how you discover you’re capable of far more than you imagined, not by pushing harder, but by working with a lighter spirit.

Here’s a simple practice to try today. Pick one task you usually find to be a chore. Before you start, take 30 seconds to connect it to a bigger purpose. Maybe responding to that difficult email is a chance to practice clear communication, or finishing a report brings clarity and order to your team. Focus on the positive outcome, not just the effort, and notice if the task itself starts to feel less like a burden.

True productivity isn't about how much you can carry; it's about learning to travel light.
