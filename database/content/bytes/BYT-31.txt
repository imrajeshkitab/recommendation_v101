It’s one of life’s great paradoxes: the most effective way to heal your own life might be to completely forget about yourself and focus on helping someone else.

Here’s the distilled wisdom on why this works.

**The Altruism Prescription.** Science shows that doing things for others is like wearing a biological bulletproof vest. In studies, people living a life of service had far more robust immune systems than affluent “pleasure seekers.” The act of giving without expecting anything in return is a powerful recipe for a longer, healthier, and happier life. When you’re feeling stuck—whether with a health issue or a financial problem—shifting your focus to send a positive intention to someone else in need can create a powerful shift. The universe seems to reward the act of selfless giving, often bringing you the exact breakthrough you were looking for.

**The Oneness Shortcut.** When people gather in small groups to focus a shared, positive thought on one person, something remarkable happens in their brains. Neuroscientists studying these groups found that in just 10 minutes, participants' brainwaves shifted dramatically. The parts of the brain responsible for feeling separate, worried, and negative quieted down significantly. Their brain activity mirrored that of master meditators in ecstatic states of prayer—a state that usually takes years of dedicated practice to achieve. This feeling of profound connection, or "oneness," dissolves the sense of isolation we normally live with and creates a powerful environment for healing.

**Your Practice This Week**

Gather a small group of friends, even just three or four, online or in person. Pick one person in the group and a specific, positive intention for them (e.g., "We intend for Sarah to have a full and rapid recovery from her cold"). For 10 minutes, have everyone quietly hold that intention, visualizing the positive outcome for that person. Afterward, take a moment to notice how you feel. You might be surprised that the sender often feels as good as the receiver.

**The Close**

Healing isn't a solo mission. It’s a collective power you unlock when you realize that lifting others up is the fastest way to rise yourself.
