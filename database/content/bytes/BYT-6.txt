 The next time you feel that wave of "too much" rising, stop. Physically take three steps back from your desk or phone. Look out a window and find one small detail you've never paid attention to before. Focus on it for just 60 seconds. Notice its color, shape, and texture without judgment.
 
 ### **Close**
 
 The problems don't magically disappear when you step back, but your ability to face them changes completely. You go from "I can't handle this" to "We'll figure it out."
