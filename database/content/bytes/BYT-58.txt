Picture the stars on a clear night. Each is a single point of light, but together they illuminate the entire sky. What if your own inner work—the effort you make to be more centered and kind—could have a similar, collective effect?

Here are a couple of insights on how cultivating your inner world can ripple outward.

First, your inner state creates an outer wave. Think of the energy you feel when you live from a place of love and compassion. It’s not just for you; it's like a wave of fresh air that enlivens everyone you interact with. Working on yourself isn't a selfish act. When you cultivate a heart-centered presence, you become a beacon, and your light joins with others to make the world a brighter place.

Second, an open heart follows a natural sequence: flexibility, generosity, and humility. It starts with being flexible in your views and letting go of rigidity. This mental spaciousness naturally gives rise to generosity—not just with things, but with your time and understanding. And when you are truly generous, it leads to humility, a quiet confidence that keeps you grounded and connected to others.

**Your Practice**

Tonight, try this simple meditation. Sit comfortably and gently close your eyes. Turn your attention to the center of your chest. Make the subtle suggestion that there is a source of light within your heart. You don't need to see it or force anything. Simply rest with the idea that this light is there, gently attracting you from within. Stay with that feeling for two to three minutes, allowing your awareness to be drawn inward.

Stillness isn’t about emptying your mind—it’s about filling your heart.
