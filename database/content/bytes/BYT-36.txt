When you hear the word 'Yoga,' you probably picture physical poses and stretches. But what if that’s just the tip of the iceberg, the entry-level course for a much deeper system designed to refine your entire being? True yoga offers a comprehensive roadmap for inner evolution, moving far beyond the mat.

### **Core Insight**

**Your growth follows a four-part roadmap.**
Yoga isn't a random collection of techniques; it's a structured path to expand your consciousness. This journey unfolds across four key stages:
1.  **Developing Discernment (`Viveka`):** This is your inner wisdom kicking in. It’s the ability to clearly see what choices truly nourish your long-term growth versus what just satisfies a temporary craving. It’s about learning to listen to your conscience over your impulses.
2.  **Finding Freedom in Detachment (`Vairagya`):** Once you can see things clearly, you naturally start to let go of unhealthy attachments. This isn't about becoming cold or uncaring; it's about releasing the desperate grip on outcomes, possessions, and approval, which frees up incredible mental and emotional energy.
3.  **Mastering Your Inner World (`Shat-Sampatti`):** This is the practical toolkit. It includes six key skills that build on each other, from cultivating a calm and focused mind (`Shama`) to developing a state of unshakable faith (`Shraddha`) and fortitude (`Titiksha`), where you can gracefully handle whatever life throws your way.
4.  **Answering the Call for More (`Mumukshutva`):** This is the deep, internal craving for liberation and purpose—the feeling that there’s more to life. This yearning is not an endpoint but the powerful fuel that propels you forward on the journey.

### **Your Practice**

Tonight, practice a moment of discernment. Before you make a simple choice—like what to watch, what to eat, or how to respond to a message—pause for just ten seconds. Quietly ask yourself, "Will this choice genuinely benefit me, or is it just an old habit?" Don't judge the answer. Simply notice what it feels like to ask the question.

### **Closing Thought**

Yoga isn't just about creating flexibility in your body; it's about developing a clear, resilient, and purposeful mind.
