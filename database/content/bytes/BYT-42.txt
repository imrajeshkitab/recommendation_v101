We spend so much time trying to teach our children how to be adults. But what if they're here to teach us how to be human again?

### Core Insight: Your Child is Your Mirror

Children rarely do what we say, but they almost always do what we do. They are powerful observers, mirroring our habits, our honesty, and our reactions. This means the most profound parenting hack isn't about finding the right discipline techniques; it's about becoming the person you hope your child will be. When you see their unfiltered joy, innocence, and curiosity, it’s not just a cute phase—it’s a reminder of the essential qualities we often lose as adults. Their presence invites you to reclaim that child-like nature within yourself.

### Your Practice: Learn from the Little Teacher

Tonight, take a moment to think about one quality you admire in a child—their uninhibited laughter, their deep curiosity, or their simple presence. Ask yourself: How can I invite a little more of that quality into my own life tomorrow? Maybe it’s putting your phone down to simply watch the clouds, or laughing without worrying what others think.

### The Takeaway

The real journey of parenthood isn’t just about raising a child—it’s about letting them raise you, too.
