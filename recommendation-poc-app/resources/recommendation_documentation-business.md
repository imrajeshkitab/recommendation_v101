
# How We Recommend Content: An Easy Guide

This guide explains how our application recommends content to you. We use a smart system to find the perfect content for your needs, and this document will walk you through how it works in simple terms.

## 1. Recommendations Through the Questionnaire

Have you ever had a friend who knows you so well they can pick out the perfect book for you? Our questionnaire works in a similar way.

### How It Works

1.  **You Answer Questions**: We start by asking you a few questions about yourself, your goals, and what you're interested in. Think of this as telling our system a little bit about yourself.

2.  **We Find Your "Keywords"**: Each time you choose an answer, our system identifies important "keywords" or "tags" that describe you. For example, if you say you're looking for a new career path, we might tag your profile with keywords like "career change" and "personal growth".

3.  **Matching You with Content**: We have a library of content, and every book, article, or video is also tagged with keywords. Our system then plays a matching game. It looks for content that has the most keywords in common with your profile.

4.  **Your Personal Recommendations**: The content that has the most matching keywords with your profile gets the highest score. We then present you with a list of these top-scoring items, which are your personalized recommendations.

**In a nutshell**: The more your keywords overlap with a content's keywords, the more likely it is to be a good fit for you.

## 2. Finding Similar Content

Have you ever finished a great book and wished you could find another one just like it? Our "Similar Content" feature does exactly that.

### How It Works

1.  **You Pick a Content You Like**: You start by selecting a piece of content that you enjoyed.

2.  **We Look at Its Keywords**: Just like with your profile, we look at the keywords associated with that content.

3.  **Finding a Match**: Our system then searches our entire library for other content that shares the most keywords with the one you selected.

4.  **Showing You Similar Content**: The content with the highest number of matching keywords is then recommended to you as "similar content".

**In a nutshell**: We find content with similar themes and topics by matching their keywords.
